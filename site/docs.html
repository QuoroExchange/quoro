How Quoro scores a token, how a buy works and what you pay.

What Quoro is

Quoro is the first AI DEX on Robinhood Chain. Paste any token to see its facts. Every launch gets a Data score from 1 to 10 that AI explains in plain words, and you buy with ETH, BNB or POL from 17 chains.

The AI explains a score from the facts on the page. It doesn’t trade for you and doesn’t tell you what to buy.

Then buy it from any of 17 chains with that chain’s own coin: ETH, BNB on BNB Chain or POL on Polygon. One signature covers the whole buy, and the token arrives in your own wallet.

Quoro never holds your money. You sign every transaction yourself, and checking a token needs no wallet.

You can sell from the same form and receive ETH, BNB or POL on any of the 17 chains.

Data score

The Data score is a number from 1 to 10 computed from public on-chain data. It describes how a launch was set up and how it trades right now.

12345678910

It is not a price call, a rating of the team or a promise of anything. A token with a high score can still lose value. Nothing here is financial advice.

How it’s counted

Two parts, each out of 10. The launch part carries 60% of the weight, the market part 40%. The result is rounded to a whole number, then any cap that applies limits it.

Launch60%
Market40%
Data score = 0.6 × Launch + 0.4 × MarketExample: 0.6 × 10 + 0.4 × 7 = 8.8, rounds to 9.

Tokens without a score

The Data score covers launches. Stock tokens have no launch to score, so their card shows price and facts without a score. Other tokens show their supply, with no score.

How buying works

Pay with the native coin of the chain your funds are on: ETH on most chains, BNB on BNB Chain, POL on Polygon. ETH already on Robinhood Chain buys the token right there. From any other chain, your payment comes over and buys the token in the same signature.

Quoro finds the route for you. If no route exists yet, for a very new token or a very small amount, you’ll see it before signing.

Before you sign

The review shows the minimum you will receive, the network fee, the bridge fee if you pay from another chain, the route fee, the creator tax if the token has one, and the price impact. If the quote changes before you sign, you see the new numbers and confirm again.

Slippage

Slippage is how far the price may move before your buy lands. The default is 3%, and you can change it in settings. If the price moves further, nothing is bought.

After you sign

On Robinhood Chain a buy arrives in about a second, from most other chains in seconds, and from Ethereum usually in under a minute. If a confirmation is slow, the buy shows as still on its way, with a link to the explorer. Don’t buy again until it arrives.

If a cross-chain buy can’t be completed, your payment is returned.

Your activity list is kept only in this browser. Clearing site data removes the list, not your tokens.

Selling

Flip the form with the arrow between the fields to sell a token you hold on Robinhood Chain. You receive ETH, BNB or POL on any of the 17 chains, on Robinhood Chain by default.

When a sale needs a permission, your wallet first asks you to allow exactly the amount you sell, then to sign the sale. That sale takes two signatures. Without a permission step, a sale takes one.

The review shows what you sell, what you will receive, the minimum received, the fees and how many signatures it takes. Creator tax applies to sales too, on tokens that have one.

If a sale to another chain can’t be completed, your funds are returned to your wallet on Robinhood Chain.

Fees

Quoro charges no fee of its own. What you pay depends on the token and the chain you pay from, and every amount is shown before you sign.

Network feeon the chain you pay from
Bridge feeonly when you pay from another chain
Route feeup to 0.25%, on some routes
Creator taxset by the token’s creator, on buys and sales of some tokens

Price impact is not a fee and doesn’t include the fees above. It is how much your own buy moves the price, and it grows with the size of the buy.

Wallets

Checking a token needs no wallet. You connect one only to buy or sell.

Connect the wallet you already use. Quoro doesn’t create wallets and never holds your money: you sign every transaction, and tokens arrive in your own wallet.

New tokens often don’t show in a wallet until you add them. After a buy, Quoro offers a button to add the token.

Updates and new features: @quoroexchange on X.

Disclaimers

The Data score and the AI explanation are readings of public on-chain data at the time shown. They are not advice and do not predict prices.

New tokens are risky and can lose all of their value. Buy only what you can afford to lose.

A signed transaction can’t be reversed, by you or by Quoro. Check the review before you sign.

Stock token availability depends on where you live. The note at the bottom of this page lists the restrictions.
